03 / Research direction

Building knowledge graphs for evolving cultural interpretation

My background in knowledge organization, metadata, and applied machine learning has led me to questions about how cultural and creative content can be represented beyond fixed categories. I hope to develop knowledge graphs and ontologies that connect content, audiences, interpretations, and temporal evidence.

01

Modeling audience reception over time

How can knowledge graphs represent how audiences interpret serialized narratives and creative works differently across contexts and over time?

02

Representing time-bound, provenance-aware statements

How can a system record who made a claim, when it was made, what evidence supports it, and how its validity changes?

03

Connecting metadata, content, and interpretation

How can knowledge organization and computational text analysis support more human-centered access to cultural and creative content?
